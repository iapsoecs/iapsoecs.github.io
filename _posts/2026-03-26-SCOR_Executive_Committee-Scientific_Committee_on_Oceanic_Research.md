## Call for Applications: Early-Career Scientist Position on the SCOR Executive Committee

The 2026 call for applications for an Early-Career Scientist (ECS) to join the SCOR Executive Committee is now open!

 
Position Description: The ECS member will have the same responsibilities as other SCOR Executive Committee members (see https://scor-int.org/scor/about/officers/job-descriptions/). The ECS member will also help reach a broader community of ECS to get involved in SCOR activities. They will build on the initiatives of prior ECS members, which have included organizing ECS activities at annual meetings, delivering presentations to ECS audiences, and recommending improved practices to SCOR. The term of appointment will be for two years.

 
Requirements: Applicants should be no more than 10 years from PhD not counting time for family leaves and should hold an ocean science-related professional position.
