We have a 3-year PostDoc position open on ocean biogeochemical modelling:

Attribution and timescales of ocean carbon sequestration:

The tasks
include assessing carbon pathways into the interior ocean and timescale of
carbon sequestration with Lagrangian tools. The project is part of the ERC
starting grant project OceanPeak. For more information, please see
https://jobs.awi.de/Vacancies/2160/Description/2

Closing date for application is 22 April 2026. Please submit a complete
application that includes a motivation letter, CV including list of
publications if applicable, copies of certificates, contact details of two
references and preferred start date (see also
https://www.awi.de/en/work-study/jobs/how-to-apply.html
). The position is
based at the Alfred Wegener Institute, Helmholtz Centre for Polar- and
Marine Research, Bremerhaven, Germany.
